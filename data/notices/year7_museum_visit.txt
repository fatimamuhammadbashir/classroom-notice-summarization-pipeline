Year 7 Science Museum Visit

Dear parents and guardians of Year 7 students,

Our Year 7 science trip to the Northbridge Science Museum will take place on Friday 5 March 2027. Students must arrive at school by 7:45 am. The coach will leave at 8:00 am and return to school at approximately 4:30 pm.

Please return the signed permission slip to the school office by Wednesday 3 March 2027. The visit costs £8, and payment must be made by Monday 1 March 2027. Students should bring a packed lunch and a water bottle. Do not include nuts or peanut products in packed lunches. Students must wear school uniform and comfortable shoes.

If you have questions, contact the Year 7 office before the visit.
