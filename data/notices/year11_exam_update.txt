Year 11 Examination Update

Dear Year 11 students and parents,

The January mock examinations begin on Monday 12 January 2027 and finish on Friday 23 January 2027. Morning exams start at 9:00 am and afternoon exams start at 1:30 pm.

Students must arrive at the exam hall 15 minutes before each session. They must bring their candidate number and two black pens. Mobile phones are not allowed in the exam hall. Any student who is ill must contact the examinations office before 8:00 am on the day of the exam.

A revised timetable is attached. Students should check their seat number on the noticeboard by Thursday 8 January 2027.
