Northbridge Book Fair

The book fair will visit the school library from Tuesday 2 March 2027 to Thursday 4 March 2027. Students in Years 5 and 6 will visit with their class. The fair will be open to families on Wednesday 3 March 2027 from 3:30 pm to 5:00 pm.

Students may bring money to buy a book, but purchases are optional. Please do not send large amounts of cash. All proceeds support the school library.
