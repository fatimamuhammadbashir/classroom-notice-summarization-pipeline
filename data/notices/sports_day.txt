Year 8 Sports Day

Year 8 Sports Day will take place on Friday 19 June 2027 at Northbridge Athletics Track. Students should arrive at school at 8:30 am. The event will finish at 3:00 pm.

Students must wear house colours and bring a water bottle, sunscreen and a hat. Please pack a named lunch. Students must not bring glass bottles. Parents are welcome to attend; please sign in at the main gate.
